We're building a "fox hunting" app for a hacker camp. This is an app targeted for 6-12 year olds that are hunting cartoon animals, from realistic to fantastical, that are hidden in the forest. Technically, these are beacons sending out RF that they can hunt with a directional antenna. The UI consists of an overview screen of the creatures they can hunt (about 12). About 3-4 will be active at any given time, the rest are "dormant".

Once they select one, they can go hunting for that one. They will go to a different screen where they can see an outline of that creature, and some kind of "heartbeat". As they get closer, the heartbeat speeds up.

Once they found the physical device they will see that it has a code. They can tap their own device (it has a touch screen) to enter the code. They then see the full creature and have unlocked it, it is then part of their inventory/library (Think Pokemon/Pokedex).

Creatures have a status: normal / rare / legendary. Normal creatures have normal names: cat, fox, etc. rare creatures have  funny combined names, e.g. (in Dutch) "everzwaan", "tijghert". legendary creatures only appear at certain times and maybe have a gold outline or something. 

The whole UI is in Dutch!

UI style is cutesy, pixel-art. Think Animal Crossing meets Pokemon

Screen is 320x240 262k colors touchscreen. Device is running MicroPythonOS.
